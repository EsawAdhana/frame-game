### How to participate (5 steps)

1. **Sign up** with your email and a password (at least 8 characters). There is no magic link.
2. **Pick a username** on the next screen — friends will find you by `@handle`.
3. **Follow everyone** in the cohort using the profile links below — open each URL and tap Follow (skip yourself).
4. **Post today's photo** from the Post tab. Until you post, other people's photos stay blurred — you have to contribute to see the collage.
5. **Like or comment** on at least one post when you're browsing.

### Timing

- A **new prompt drops every day at 12:00 PM Pacific**.
- Post before noon PT if you want to be part of that day's collage.

### What we're measuring

We're interested in how people show up for a daily shared ritual — not testing your photography. Any phone photo that fits the prompt is perfect.
